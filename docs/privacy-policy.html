Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 4, 2026

🛡️ Family-Safe & COPPA Compliant

Welcome to Zayn Storybook ("we", "our", or "us"). We create animated educational children's storybooks and publish family-safe video content across digital platforms including YouTube, Facebook, Instagram, and TikTok.

This Privacy Policy explains how our applications, tools, and social media integrations handle user data.

1. Information Collection and Use

Zayn Storybook is committed to protecting your privacy:

  • No Personal Data Collection: Our application does not collect, record, harvest, sell, or share personal identifiable information (PII) such as your name, email address, physical address, or phone number.
  • Platform Analytics: We only access aggregated, anonymized analytics provided by YouTube Data API and Meta Graph API (e.g., aggregate view counts, likes, and comments) to track content quality and performance.

2. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Our content is exclusively family-friendly and educational:

  • We strictly adhere to the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).
  • We do not knowingly solicit, collect, or process personal data from children under the age of 13.

3. Third-Party Services & APIs

Our publishing tools interact with trusted platform APIs under their respective privacy policies:

  • Google & YouTube: Google Privacy Policy and YouTube Terms of Service
  • Meta Platforms (Facebook & Instagram): Meta Privacy Policy
  • TikTok: TikTok Privacy Policy

4. User Data Deletion Instructions

Zayn Storybook does not maintain external user databases or store your personal data on our servers. If you wish to remove any interaction (likes or comments) made on our channel or pages, you can delete them directly through your YouTube, Facebook, or Instagram account.

If you have any data removal requests or inquiries regarding our app integrations, please email us directly.
