# Privacy Policy for Zayn Storybook

**Effective Date:** November 4, 2026

Welcome to **Zayn Storybook** ("we", "our", or "us"). We create animated educational children's storybooks and publish video content across digital platforms including Facebook, Instagram, and YouTube.

This Privacy Policy explains how our applications and social media integrations handle user data.

---

## 1. Information Collection and Use
Zayn Storybook is committed to protecting your privacy:
- **No Personal Data Collection:** Our application does not collect, record, harvest, sell, or share personal identifiable information (PII) such as your name, email address, physical address, or phone number.
- **Platform Analytics:** We only access aggregated, anonymized analytics provided by Meta Graph API and YouTube Data API (e.g., aggregate view counts, likes, and comments) to track content performance.

## 2. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)
Our content is family-friendly:
- We strictly adhere to the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).
- We do not knowingly solicit, collect, or process personal data from children under the age of 13.

## 3. Third-Party Services & APIs
Our publishing tools interact with trusted platform APIs:
- **Meta Platforms (Facebook & Instagram):** [https://www.facebook.com/privacy/policy](https://www.facebook.com/privacy/policy)
- **Google / YouTube:** [https://policies.google.com/privacy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)

## 4. User Data Deletion Instructions
Zayn Storybook does not maintain external user databases or store your personal data on our servers. 
If you wish to remove any interaction (likes or comments) made on our Facebook Page or Instagram profile, you can delete them directly through your Facebook or Instagram account.

If you have any data removal requests or inquiries regarding our Meta app integration, please email us directly.
